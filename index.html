1Overview

Speaker Turbo Cleaner ("the App") is developed and published by Siddi Furqan (referred to as "we", "us", or "the developer" throughout this policy), reachable at siddifurqan@gmail.com. The App plays sequences of audio tones at varying frequencies, intended to help loosen dust and eject trapped water from your phone's speaker grille, paired with an on-screen turbine/gauge animation and vibration feedback. This policy applies to all users of the App on Android.

2Information we collect

We collect the minimum information necessary for the App to function:

Data typeWhy we access itLeaves your device?

We do not run our own backend servers that store, log, or transmit your usage data. Everything the App tracks (cleaning history, total cleans) stays on your device.

3App permissions explained

  • Vibration — used to give haptic feedback when a cleaning cycle finishes. No data is collected through this permission.
  • Internet access — required to load advertisements via Google AdMob. The App's core tone-playback and cleaning-cycle features work without an internet connection.

The App does not request Camera, Microphone, Location, Contacts, Photos, or Storage permissions.

4Information we do NOT collect

  • No account creation, login, username, or password is required to use the App.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any other personal identifiers.
  • We do not collect precise or approximate location data.
  • We do not access your microphone, camera, photos, or contacts.
  • We do not sell your data to anyone, under any circumstances.

5Third-party services (Google AdMob)

Speaker Turbo Cleaner displays advertisements using Google AdMob, a service provided by Google LLC. AdMob may collect and process device identifiers (such as the Advertising ID), approximate region-level information, and app interaction data in order to serve and measure advertisements.

This processing is governed by Google's own privacy policy and advertising policies, not by us directly. We encourage you to review:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use AdMob

You can manage ad personalization and reset your Advertising ID at any time through your device's system settings (typically under Settings → Google → Ads on Android).

6Where your data is stored

Your cleaning history and cycle statistics are stored locally on your device only, using standard Android app storage. This data is not synced to any cloud service operated by us, and it is automatically removed if you uninstall the App or clear its storage.

7Data sharing & sale

We do not share your personal data with third parties for their own marketing purposes, and we do not sell your data. The only data shared with a third party is the Advertising ID shared with Google AdMob, solely for the purpose of displaying and measuring in-app advertisements.

8Health & device safety disclaimer

Speaker Turbo Cleaner is provided for general convenience purposes only. Results in removing dust or moisture from your speaker vary by device model, speaker design, and the severity of water exposure. The App is not a certified waterproofing or repair tool, and we make no guarantee that using it will fully restore speaker function or prevent water damage. If your device has sustained significant water exposure, consider powering it off and consulting the manufacturer or a qualified repair technician. Use of audio tones at high volume for extended periods may be uncomfortable for some users — lower your device volume if needed.

9Your choices & controls

  • You can clear your entire cleaning history at any time from the App's Settings screen.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising via your device's Google Ads settings.
  • Uninstalling the App removes all locally stored data associated with it.

10Children's privacy

Speaker Turbo Cleaner is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us using the details below and we will take appropriate steps to address it.
